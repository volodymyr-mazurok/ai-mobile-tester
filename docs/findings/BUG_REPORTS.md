# Bug reports

Tracker-ready restatements of entries in [APP_ISSUES.md](APP_ISSUES.md) - written for
somebody who does not know this framework and will never open it.

Separate from `APP_ISSUES.md` on purpose. That file is a working log, ordered by
discovery and full of framework vocabulary. This one is prose an app developer can
act on: what they do, what they should see, what they see instead.

**Format.** `## <id>. <summary>` - the same id as `APP_ISSUES.md`, so the two never
drift apart.

**Not every finding gets one.** A finding is promoted here when somebody decides to
report it. Until then it lives in `APP_ISSUES.md` and nowhere else.
